Emil Regard helps companies turn new technologies into services customers buy and systems people can use.

Across 30 years in satellite communications and maritime, his work has spanned large public companies and startups in satellite, space and maritime.

He founded, built and sold BlueTide Communications, growing its offering from satellite connectivity into managed IT and cybersecurity. He also built the maritime installation and services-partner network that put those technologies to work wherever customers operated.

At KVH Industries, he served as VP, Managed Services & Strategic Services Growth, taking a new managed IT, cybersecurity and compliance platform from business case to market. Earlier leadership roles at Hughes Network Systems and Loral Space & Communications add experience in channel development and bringing satellite services to new markets.

Today, 30 North brings that combination of commercial judgment, technical understanding and field experience to companies launching new technology—and to operators ready to use it.
